OFFICIAL TOURNAMENT RULES

Official tournament mechanics, property trading, even building, and bankruptcy.

Properties & Auctions

Landing on an unowned street, railroad, or utility allows the player to purchase it directly from the Bank at face value. If the player declines to purchase, the property is immediately offered in an open public auction where all active players may bid.

Even Building Rule (Houses & Hotels)

Houses may only be erected once a player holds full monopoly ownership of a color group. Construction must be distributed evenly across all properties in the set: no street may receive a second house until every street in that color group possesses one house. 4 Houses may be converted into 1 Hotel by paying the hotel price.

Incarceration & Escape

A player is sent to Jail upon landing on 'Go to Jail', rolling three consecutive doubles, or drawing a jail transit card. No $200 salary is awarded when sent to Jail. While imprisoned, players still collect normal rent. Escape is achieved by rolling doubles on any of 3 turns, paying a $50 fine prior to rolling, or using a Get Out of Jail Free card.

Mortgages & Bankruptcy

Unimproved properties can be mortgaged to the Bank for 50% face value. Mortgaged properties produce zero rent. Unmortgaging requires paying the mortgage principal plus 10% interest. If total liquidated assets cannot satisfy debt owed to a creditor, the debtor declares bankruptcy and all properties and cash transfer directly to that creditor.

NEURAL ENGINE & AI ARCHITECTURES

Technical specifications for the reinforcement learning and algorithmic bots.

Politically Perfect Organism (RL)

Deep Reinforcement Learning agent trained via Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) over 100,000 simulated games. Features an Actor-Critic architecture with 106-dimensional normalized observation vector encoding board status, relative cash equity, monopoly completion indexes, and jail states.

Cash Elo (Chess Grandmaster)

Algorithmic tournament engine computing Expected Value (EV) distributions and defensive monopoly blockade scores. Maintains dynamic cash liquidity safety buffers ($300-$600) to mitigate bankruptcy risks while evaluating bilateral trades through comparative rent yield differentials.

Moneybags Markov (Markov Model)

Stochastic engine utilizing a 40x40 discrete-time transition probability matrix factoring dice probability distributions, jail transit absorption, and Chance cards. Aggressively prioritizes acquisition of high-traffic zones (Orange, Red, and Railroad groups) to maximize yield per lap.

Ollama Diplomatic Brain

Zero-shot local LLM integration generating context-rich table dialogue, trade negotiations, and post-game debriefs. Feeds live board state metrics, player financial standings, and reciprocal favor history into local models (e.g. LLaMA-3) to produce bilateral proposals and counteroffers.
